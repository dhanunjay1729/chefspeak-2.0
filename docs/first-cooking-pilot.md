# First cooking pilot

Goal: observe whether spoken guidance in a comfortable language helps someone finish a real meal and return to cook again. Recruit 5–10 people who can cook within the next week, including beginners and people who prefer different supported languages.

## Invitation draft

> I'm testing ChefSpeak, which reads recipe steps aloud in your preferred language. Could you use it to cook one meal this week and tell me where you got stuck? Pick something you actually want to eat. It would help if you could share whether the spoken steps were clear and whether you would use it for another meal.
>
> https://chefspeak.vercel.app

This is a draft for the owner to send to chosen participants; no invitations have been sent.

## Before cooking

- Ask which dish they plan to cook, which language they want, and what they normally use for cooking guidance.
- Have them check ingredients, quantities and dietary needs before starting.
- Ask them to choose their preferred language and use their normal phone/browser.
- Give each session an anonymous ID. Record only the information needed to understand product use.

## During cooking

- Observe whether they can start narration, move between steps, repeat a step, pause/resume, and use timers.
- Record any unclear pronunciation, awkward translation, incorrect quantity, audio failure or navigation confusion.
- Record when they need the founder's help and what caused the interruption.
- Ask whether they finished the meal and whether the result was usable.

## After cooking

Ask: What was most useful? Where did you switch to another source? Was anything hard to understand? What would make you choose this again? After a week, record actual return use and the reason for returning or stopping.

Review completion, assistance, language comprehension and repeat use alongside the TTS usage logs. Fix repeated blockers first. This small pilot provides learning; it does not establish product-market fit, willingness to pay, or a reliable cost forecast for 1,000 users.
